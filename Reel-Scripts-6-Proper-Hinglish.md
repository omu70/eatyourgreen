### Production notes
- Hook must land in the **first 2 seconds** — open on the child/food or Prerna's face, never an intro.
- Keep captions large and high-contrast; place them in the top two-thirds (Reels UI covers the bottom).
- Background: soft, real home sound or trending low-volume audio; let the voice lead.
- One CTA only per reel (link in bio OR a comment keyword: GREEN / BOOK2 / START).
- Cold-reach picks: Videos 1, 2, 3, 6. Warm/retarget: 4, 5.
