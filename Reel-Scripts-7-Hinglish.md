# Eat Your Green — 7 Hinglish Reel Scripts
Structure for every script: Hook (0–4s) → Retention → Problem → Benefit + CTA. Length: 30–35 sec (~80 words).
Voice: warm, real Indian mom / Prerna to camera. On-screen captions ON (most watch on mute). One idea per reel.
Prepared by Dizigroww.

## 1. Target: Mum of a 2–6 yr old who fights at every meal → The Guide (₹399)
- **HOOK (0–4s)** — on-screen: *"Roz ki sabzi ki ladai?"*
  "Agar har dinner ek jung ban jaata hai — ye 30 second sirf aapke liye hain."
- **Retention (4–12s):** "Main bhi yahi galti kar rahi thi… zyada zor, zyada bribe, zyada drama."
- **Problem (12–22s):** "Par jitna hum push karte hain, bachcha utna hi 'nahi' bolta hai — kyunki pressure se sabzi dushman ban jaati hai."
- **Benefit + CTA (22–34s):** "Eat Your Green Guide ek gentle 4C method sikhata hai — bina chillaaye, bina bribe. 7 din me pehli chhoti 'haan'. Link bio me."

### Quick filming notes
- Say the hook in the first 2 seconds; don't "intro" yourself first.
- Keep captions on screen, big and readable, since most watch muted.
- One CTA per reel only (link in bio OR a comment keyword like GREEN/START).
- Reels 1, 3, 4, 7 are strongest for cold reach; 2, 5, 6 work great for warm/retargeting.
- Swap in real B-roll: real dinner moments, the book/printables, a child trying a green.
